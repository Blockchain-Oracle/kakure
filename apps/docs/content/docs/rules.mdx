## Windows

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Assets | BTC, ETH, SOL, ZEC, XRP, DOGE |
| Lengths | 1m, 2m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h, 1d |
| Series | 2m and 5m run two series each, one minute and three minutes apart; the rest run one |
| Entry | Until 30 seconds before the close; after that, positions ride to settlement |

A Window **opens** at its start and **closes** at its end. Before it opens, both sides are priced at 0.5 (pays about
1.90×), since nobody knows the opening price yet. Once it opens, it trades as soon as two of its three exchanges
have committed their opening minute; the **Line** is the median of those opening prints.

Each asset is printed by three exchanges: Coinbase, Kraken and Bitstamp (for ZEC: Coinbase, Kraken and Gemini). A
**print** is the close of that exchange's 1-minute candle ending at the moment in question.

## The price

The house prices every Window from one public formula, on the same price stream the chart draws:

```
P(Up) = Φ( ln(S / O) / (σ · √(max(τ, 5 s) / 1 year)) )
P(Down) = 1 − P(Up)
```

- **S** is the live price, **O** the Line, **τ** the time to the close, **Φ** the standard normal distribution.
- **σ** is each asset's annualised volatility from 1-minute log returns over the last 300 minutes (from the first
  voting exchange), clamped to 20 %–250 % and published signed every minute.
- **Ask** = P plus a half-spread of 2.5 points; **bid** = P minus 2.5 points. A side is sold only while its ask is
  at most 98 and bought back only while its bid is at least 2; outside that it's refused, never clamped.
- **Pays ×** = 1 ÷ ask. There is no fee beyond the spread.
- A payout is stake ÷ ask, rounded down to the smallest chip (0.00001); a close pays payout × bid, rounded down the
  same way.

The house refuses to quote when its price stream has been silent for more than 3 seconds or an asset's last tick is
older than 15 seconds, and it ignores any tick stamped in the future.

**Slippage.** Your order carries the most you'll pay: the quote plus your tolerance (2 % by default; 0.5, 1, 2 or
5 %) plus a cushion of 1 point. A worse ask is refused as **Price moved**.

## Limits

| | Testnet | Mainnet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stake per trade | 0.001 to 1 | 0.001 to 0.05 |
| Most the house pays on one Window side | 10 | 0.5 |
| Most one parlay pays | 5 | 0.2 |
| Most one band pays | 5 | 0.2 |

The side limit counts a Window side's open payout in quarters of the limit, rounded up, so a side can fill to
between three quarters and all of it.

## Settlement

The operator's minute work runs 7 seconds into each minute. A Window settles at the first one after its close
once all three exchanges have committed their closing minute, or, if one is late, after a 10-minute grace
without it.

- Each exchange votes **Up** if its closing print is above its opening print, **Down** if below, and casts no vote if
  either print is missing or they're equal.
- **Two votes of three decide.** Anything else refunds every position its stake, as does a Window that never got
  its Line.
- Prices are compared as exact decimals, never rounded.

## Positions

**Up / Down.** Pays its payout if its side wins, its stake on a refund, nothing otherwise. Close or reduce it at the
bid while the Window trades. A reduce sells a share of the payout (rounded down to the chip) for the same share of
the stake; if less than one chip would remain, it closes the whole position.

**Exits.** An Up or Down position can carry a trailing stop (0.1 % to 10 % behind the best price since you armed it),
a stop-loss price and a take-profit bid. **The mint watches them, not your device,** at least once a second, so they
fire with your tab closed; they act only while the Window trades, and a position still armed at the close is redeemed
for you at settlement.

**Parlays.** Two or three Up/Down legs on different Windows, each with at least 30 seconds of entry left. The
combined price is the product of the legs' asks (when two legs close at the same moment, at least half the lower
ask, since they move together), refused under 1 %. Every leg must win; a lost leg loses it all; a refunded leg drops
out and the rest are re-priced; if every leg is refunded, the stake comes back. A parlay can't be sold back.

**Bands** (the Range and Moonshot games). A band has a lower edge, an upper edge, or both, and a side: **inside** or
**outside**. P(inside) = P(close above the lower edge) − P(close above the upper edge), priced and limited like
Up/Down, on the live price before and after the open. It settles on two of three closing prints falling inside
(edges included) or outside. Bands can be closed and reduced at the bid; they carry no exits. Range's presets are
±$15, ±$30 and ±$55 on BTC over 5 minutes, scaled by the square root of time and by price; Moonshot solves the edge
so the ask is 1 ÷ your reach (×2, ×3, ×5, ×10 or ×25).

## Getting paid

A settled position is redeemed for fresh chips, blind-signed like your deposit's. With the app open this happens
on its own shortly after settlement; a position armed with an exit is redeemed by the mint at settlement and its
chips wait for your device to collect them.
