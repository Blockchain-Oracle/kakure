Every market's exact wording, sources, cutoff, refund cases and payout formula are fixed and signed **before**
betting opens, and its rules hash is published so anyone can check they never changed (see [Verify](/verify)).

## Tickets

- Sizes: 0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5 or 1 ZEC. These are ZIP 318's canonical values (1, 2 or 5 × 10^k).
- One payment is one ticket. The memo names the market, side (`U`/`D`, or `Y`/`N` for events), size, a 16-byte
  random nonce and your payout address.
- A ticket's hash is BLAKE2b-256 with personalisation `Kakure_Ticket_v1` over: the network byte (mainnet `0x01`,
  testnet `0x02`), the market id's length and bytes, the side, the size in zatoshi (8 bytes, little-endian) and the
  nonce. Side and size sit inside the hash, so the published list of hashes reveals neither.

## When betting closes: the cutoff block

Betting closes 10 minutes before the market closes. The **cutoff block H_c** is the first block whose timestamp is
at or after the cutoff time, taken once it has 10 confirmations. Bets mined from the first block at or after
betting opened, up to but not including H_c, count. H_c is published in the bundle once final. Miners set block
timestamps within consensus limits; that latitude is disclosed, not hidden.

## How a close market resolves: the direction vote

Three venues vote. For BTC, ETH, SOL, DOGE and XRP they are Coinbase Exchange, Kraken and Bitstamp; for ZEC,
Coinbase Exchange, Kraken and Gemini. Each venue votes **Up**, **Down** or **Flat** by comparing the close of its
committed 1-hour candle ending at the window's end with its **opening reference**: the close of its 1-hour candle
ending at the window's start, recorded and signed before betting opens. Up or Down wins with at least 2 of the 3
votes. A missing or zero-volume candle is no vote.

Event markets resolve from chain data instead; their rules say exactly how (for example, a block height's
median-time-past, read from public light-wallet servers, with 10 confirmations).

## Odds

A market's ticket count, its list of ticket hashes and its totals are published only in groups: each change needs at
least 3 new tickets since the last one, so no single bet shows. Odds stay **sealed** (hidden) until each side has at
least 3 tickets; until then only the count is published, in the same groups. A signed batch goes out every 10
minutes regardless.

When the cutoff block is final, any tickets still waiting are published in a last group, but only if there are at
least 3 of them. **Fewer than 3 are refunded in full and never published**, so a bet that arrives last, alone,
doesn't play.

## Payouts

A winning ticket of size *s* receives

```
s + round( s × L × (10000 − 200) / (W × 10000) )
```

zatoshi, where *W* and *L* are the winning and losing pools at the cutoff, rounded to the nearest 100,000 zatoshi
(0.001 ZEC, half up). The **2 % fee comes from the losing side only**, and the house absorbs the rounding in either
direction. Every same-size winning ticket is paid the same amount.

Each payout is split into **canonical notes** (1, 2 or 5 × 10^k zatoshi, largest first), and each note is sent as
its own transaction at a random time **2 to 6 hours after resolution**. Kakure publishes coarse status only
("markets resolved before T: all sent by T′"), never payout transaction ids.

## Refunds

Every ticket in a market is refunded to its payout address if, at the cutoff:

- either side has fewer than 3 tickets;
- no direction gets 2 of the 3 venue votes (close markets), or the light-wallet servers disagree on the best chain
  (event markets);
- fewer than 2 venues gave an opening reference (close markets).

Single tickets are refunded when they were in a last group of fewer than 3 (see Odds), mined at or after H_c, mined before betting opened, paid with a wrong
amount of 0.001 ZEC or more (smaller amounts are kept as dust), named a side the market doesn't have, or repeated a
nonce (the first, by block height and transaction index, counts).

A missing or malformed memo, a memo for the other network, or a payout address without an Orchard receiver leaves
**no usable return address**: those funds can't be returned. The bet builder makes all three impossible.
