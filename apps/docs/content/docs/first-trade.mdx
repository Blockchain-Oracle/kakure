<Steps>
<Step>

### Deposit once

Open the terminal and tap **Deposit**. Pick an amount (0.01 to 1 TAZ on testnet) and the app shows a QR code with
the deposit's memo already in it. Scan it with Zodl or Zingo and pay exactly that amount from shielded funds.

The deposit's id is drawn on your device and saved before the code appears, so closing the sheet loses nothing.
After **2 confirmations on testnet (3 on mainnet)** the mint signs your chips blind, the app checks every signature
against the mint's published keys, and the chips land in your device's vault (with sound on, a short bell rings).

The code carries your deposit's id: whoever has it could claim the chips. It's built on your device, never sent
anywhere, and blurs when you look away. Don't share or screenshot it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick a Window and a side

A **Window** is a stretch of time on one asset, from its open to its close. The **Line** is the asset's price at the
open, taken from the exchanges. Tap **UP** if you think it closes above the Line, **DOWN** if below. Each side shows
what it **pays** at the house's live price: pays 1.8× means a 0.1 stake returns 0.18 if you're right.

The price moves with every tick. If it moves past your slippage before your order lands (2 % by default, in the
order settings), the order is refused with **Price moved**, and nothing is spent: tap again to take the new price.

</Step>
<Step>

### Manage it, or let it ride

While the Window trades, your position is valued live at the house's bid:

- **Close** sells it back now. **Reduce** sells part of it.
- On an Up or Down position, **Trail** arms a trailing stop and **TP / SL** set a take-profit and a stop-loss. These
  run at the mint, so they fire even with your tab closed.
- A parlay can't be sold back: it rides to its last leg's close.
- In the last 30 seconds a Window stops taking trades and every position rides to the close.

</Step>
<Step>

### Settlement

About seven seconds into the minute after the Window closes, the operator reads each exchange's closing price. Two
of three agreeing above the Line means UP won; two below, DOWN won; anything else refunds every position its stake.
With the app open, winnings and refunds come back as fresh chips shortly after; otherwise the next time you open
it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Withdraw

Tap **Withdraw**, paste a fresh shielded address from your wallet's Receive screen, and pick an amount (from 0.01, in
steps of 0.001). The mint splits it into standard 1, 2 and 5 notes and sends each at its own random time within about
half an hour, so the payments can't be matched to your trades.

</Step>
</Steps>

Every step above goes through Kakure's Oblivious HTTP relay: it sees your IP but not what you sent, and the operator
sees what you sent but not your IP. See [the privacy model](/privacy).
