You need a Zcash wallet with shielded funds: **Zingo** on testnet; **Zodl** or **Zingo** on mainnet. On testnet,
get free TAZ first (see [Networks](/networks)).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Pick a market and a side

Open a market in the app and choose **Bet Up** or **Bet Down** (or Yes/No on an event). Betting closes 10
minutes before the market's close.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose one fixed ticket

Tickets come in fixed sizes only: 0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5 or 1 ZEC (TAZ on testnet). There's no amount
field on purpose: every ticket of a size looks like every other on the chain and in the pools.

</Step>
<Step>

### Paste a fresh payout address

In your wallet, open **Receive** and copy a fresh shielded (Unified) address. Paste it into the bet sheet. Your
browser checks it on the spot: it must be for the right network, have an Orchard receiver, and have no transparent
receiver. Winnings or refunds go to this address; Kakure never asks for anything else.

</Step>
<Step>

### Scan the QR with your wallet

Tap **Make QR**. Your ticket is saved in this browser first, then the code appears. Scan it with your wallet, or
tap **Open in Zingo** on the same device. Pay exactly the amount shown and don't edit the memo: it carries your
market, side, ticket size, a random nonce and your payout address.

</Step>
<Step>

### Watch it get counted

After your payment is mined, the next signed batch (one goes out every 10 minutes) lists your ticket's hash. Your
portfolio then shows it as **Counted**. Don't pay twice: if you're unsure whether it went through, check your
wallet first.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What the QR holds

The QR is a standard ZIP 321 payment request built on your device: one of the market's published addresses, the
ticket amount, and the memo `kakure:v1;n=…;m=…;s=…;z=…;k=…;p=…`. It's never sent to a server, which is also why it
works like a bearer secret: anyone who sees it learns your side and payout address. The app hides it whenever its
window isn't in front.

## In the Kakure app

The phone app has its own shielded wallet, so a bet is one tap there. Next to every web QR there's a second, smaller
code that only names the market: scan it with the Kakure app to bet on the same market from your phone.
