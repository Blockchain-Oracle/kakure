## Pay from Ironwood

Since the NU6.3 upgrade (mainnet block 3,428,143), new shielded value on Zcash lives in the **Ironwood** pool; the
older Orchard pool can only be spent from. A payment that moves money from one pool to another shows its amount on
chain. So pay your ticket from funds that are already in Ironwood: then the bet is a same-pool shielded payment that
hides sender, recipient and amount.

**Zodl users:** Zodl 3.9.0 added migration into Ironwood. Migrate first, then bet. The bet sheet reminds you.

## Your payout address

Kakure pays winnings and refunds to the address in your bet's memo. It must:

- be a **Unified Address** (`u1…` on mainnet, `utest1…` on testnet) for the network you're betting on;
- contain an **Orchard receiver**, so the payout lands in the shielded pool;
- contain **no transparent receiver**, which would make the payout public.

The app checks all three in your browser before it builds the QR, with the same rules the operator applies when it
reads your memo. A payout address without an Orchard receiver leaves no usable return address, so the bet builder
won't let you make one.

Use a **fresh** address for each bet (your wallet's Receive screen gives you a new one). It keeps your bets from
being linked to each other by anyone who learns one of them.
