## My ticket says "Waiting for its batch"

It's counted once 3 new tickets arrive, and refunded in full if the market closes first. Tickets are listed in
groups so that no single bet shows, so a paid ticket can wait a few batches (one goes out every 10 minutes). The site
can't see your wallet either: check there that the payment went out. **Don't pay twice.**

## My ticket says "Not counted"

Its market's cutoff block is final and no signed batch listed it. If you didn't pay, nothing was lost. If you paid,
it's refunded to your payout address: a late payment (mined at or after the cutoff block), a payment of the wrong
amount (unless it's under 0.001 ZEC, which is kept as dust), and a last group of fewer than 3 tickets are all
refunded under the rules.

## The payout address is refused

- **"That isn't a Unified Address"**: copy the shielded `u1…` (or `utest1…`) address from your wallet's Receive
  screen, not a transparent `t…` one.
- **"That address is for the other network"**: switch the app or your wallet so both are on the same network.
- **"No Orchard receiver"**: use a fresh Unified Address from Zodl or Zingo.
- **"Includes a transparent receiver"**: use a shielded-only address.
- **"Another of your tickets already pays out to this address"**: each bet needs a fresh address, or the operator
  could link your bets. Copy a new one from your wallet's Receive screen.
- **"Your tickets are locked"**: unlock them in Settings → Ticket lock, so the address can be checked against them.

## The app shows an error instead of markets

Each error names its fix:

- **You're offline / can't reach the data host:** reconnect and retry; your last verified data stays on screen.
- **Not signed by the Kakure operator:** the files don't match the key built into the app, so nothing is shown.
  Reload to get the current app.
- **This build has no operator key:** that network isn't open in this build yet.
- **No markets published yet:** the operator hasn't published a batch for this network; the page checks again on
  its own.

## The QR won't scan

Turn the screen brightness up and hold steady. If your camera struggles with the dotted code, tap **Trouble scanning? Use a square
code**. On the same phone, use **Open in Zingo** instead of scanning.

## I switched networks and my tickets disappeared

Each network keeps its own tickets. Switch back to see them.
