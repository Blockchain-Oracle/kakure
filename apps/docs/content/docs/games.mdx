The games hall is at **/games**. Range and Moonshot are trades priced by the house (see
[Rules and formulas](/rules)); the three below cost nothing and never touch your chips.

| Game | Stake | Scored on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Practice: Beat the coin | Free | The live price, 30 seconds after each swipe |
| Line Rider | Free | The arcade service's replay of your run |
| Candle Hop | Free | The arcade service's replay of your run |
| Lucky | Your stake | One ordinary trade, settled like any other |

## Practice: Beat the coin

Five live Windows are dealt, soonest-closing first, each with at least two minutes left. Swipe up if you think the
price will be higher in 30 seconds, down if lower. The coin calls the same cards: its call is a fixed function of the
round's seed and the card, so it's a coin flip and nothing more.

- Each card is scored on the live price exactly **30 seconds after your own swipe**, never on the Window's settlement.
- A move to the same price is **flat**: nobody's card.
- A card whose price couldn't be read at its close stays **unscored**; it's never given a number.

Practice runs entirely in your browser. Nothing about a round is sent anywhere; the tally of rounds you've played and
won stays on your device for the hall's achievements.

## Lucky

Three reels draw a live **asset**, a **side** (Up or Down) and a **reach** (×2, ×3, ×5, ×10 or ×25); you pick the
stake from four chips. The machine then deals the drawn asset's Window whose drawn side pays closest to the reach, at
the live price from the public formula, and shows that quote. Slide the lever to place it: one ordinary trade from your
chips, through the same sealed path as the terminal, settled at the Window's close.

- **Drawn on your device.** The draw is HMAC-SHA256, keyed by the hash of the latest signed bundle (the operator's
  `latest.json` names it; nobody knows it before that minute is published), over the policy, a hash of the candidate
  Windows and a seed your browser makes at the spin. **Proof** shows each input and recomputes the draw.
- **Nothing is sent until you place it.** A server-dealt draw would learn your bet before it's a bet, so there is no
  draw server. Your draws, placed or not, are listed on your device only, each with what its Window decided.
- A Window needs a minute and a half left to be drawn, so there's time to slide before entry closes 30 seconds out.

## The arcade: Line Rider and Candle Hop

Both games ride a **tape**: twelve hours of one asset's 1-minute closes (BTC or ETH, picked by the run's seed), cut
from Kakure's signed hourly prints files and checked against the operator's key before use. Line Rider turns the
minute-to-minute moves into a slope to sled; Candle Hop turns each minute into a candle to fly between.

- **Line Rider.** Hold to press into the line and build a charge, let go to hop, lean ← → to flip. Clean landings,
  flips and gaps cleared build the combo; a bad landing ends the run.
- **Candle Hop.** Tap or Space to lift the coin. Hitting a body, a wick or the floor ends the run.
- **Calmer ramp.** A slower speed ramp; runs played with it are marked on the boards.

### How a score is checked

1. When you press Play, the arcade service issues a **seed** and the tape it rides. A seed is good for one score,
   within an hour of being issued (the longest run, 30 minutes, plus 30 to start and post it).
2. Your browser records every input by the engine's tick (60 a second) and, when the run ends, sends the seed, the
   score, the length and the inputs, signed by your device's key.
3. The service **replays** the same engine over the same seed, tape and inputs. The score counts only if the replay
   reproduces it to the tick. It can't prove a person rather than a script played, so the boards say "server-checked".
4. Each nickname may post 120 runs an hour.

## Nicknames and boards

A nickname is 3–16 letters, digits, `_` or `-`, case-insensitively unique. It's bound to an Ed25519 key your browser
makes for the arcade; claiming one takes a small proof of work instead of a captcha, so nothing third-party loads.

- The boards show each nickname's best run, today, this week or all time, with the **day** it was played and never a
  time. No address, wallet or IP is ever attached.
- The key and nickname live in this browser. **Forget this device** clears them; the nickname then can't be posted to
  from here again.

## What's kept where

| | Kept by |
| --- | --- |
| Nicknames, device public keys, runs (game, score, inputs' hash, day) | The arcade service |
| Your arcade key, your runs list, Practice's tally, Lucky's draws, your streaks and achievements | This browser |
| Your IP | Nobody on the arcade's side: the site forwards only the request's method, path and body |

The arcade's calls aren't sealed like trades: the site sees them with your IP, though it keeps no logs. See
[The privacy model](/privacy).
