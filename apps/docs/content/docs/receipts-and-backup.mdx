## Where your tickets live

Only on your device. The web app keeps them in your browser's IndexedDB, encrypted with AES-GCM under a key the
browser won't let any code read out, with a separate key and store per network. There's no account and no server
copy: if you clear this browser's data without an export, the tickets are gone (payouts still go to the addresses
in your bets).

## Receipts

Open a ticket in the portfolio to see it as a perforated slip. Receipts are made on your device from your ticket and
the signed bundle, and they're **redacted by default**: no payout address, no transaction id, no bet time.

- **Outcome slip:** the market, side, size and result. It can't be checked against the signed list, and it can't
  be traced to your payment.
- **Provable:** adds your ticket's nonce, so anyone with the link can recompute the ticket hash and find it in the
  signed list. Share it only with someone you want to prove the bet to.

Each receipt can be saved as a PNG, emailed (your mail app opens with a short summary; attach the PNG), or shared
as a `/verify#…` link.

## Export and import

**Settings → Backup.** Enter a passphrase of at least 8 characters, then **Export**: you get a `.kakure` file with
this network's tickets, encrypted with AES-GCM-256 under a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA-256,
310,000 iterations). It includes payout addresses and nonces, so keep it like a wallet backup. **Import** takes the
same file and passphrase on another device. A file from the other network is refused: switch networks first.

## Forget this device

**Settings → Forget this device** erases everything Kakure stored in this browser: the ticket store and its keys,
settings, the cached public files and the service worker. It asks you to slide to confirm. Export first if you want
to keep your tickets.
