## What the app checks on every load

- `latest.json` and the bundle it names carry the operator's **Ed25519** signature, checked against the key built into
  the app (see [file formats](/file-formats)). Nothing unsigned is ever shown.
- The bundle's SHA-256 matches the hash `latest.json` names, and its sequence number matches too.
- Every chip the mint signs comes with a **DLEQ proof** against its published key for that amount, checked on your
  device before the chip is kept, so the mint can't sign your chips with a key meant to mark you.
- Every position certificate is checked against the operator key before it's saved.

## Verify: your positions

The **Verify** page checks every position on this device and sends nothing anywhere:

- **The certificate's signature** against the operator key.
- **Its price against what you accepted:** the signed ask (or a parlay's price) is no more than the limit your order
  carried.
- **The payout's arithmetic:** stake ÷ ask gives the signed payout. A position you partly closed is replayed from its
  first signed terms through each reduce, which must land on the terms signed last.
- **Parlays:** the combined price from the legs' asks, and the payout.
- **Its result,** recomputed from the signed prints: the Line from the opening prints, then two of three closing
  votes, for Up/Down; two of three closing prints inside or outside the edges, for bands; each leg, for parlays.
- **Its prints against the minute closes:** each print must be the exchange's 1-minute close signed in the hourly
  files for the minute ending at the open or the close. The operator never revises a minute once recorded, so they
  agree exactly.
- **What was paid** against what was due.

A Window that settled more than 10 minutes ago has left the minute bundle; Verify recomputes it from its hour's
prints file, which is published 12 minutes after that hour ends, and says so until then.

## Proof: the public record

The **Proof** page shows the signed record anyone can check:

- the latest bundle's signatures and hash, and **the link to the bundle before it**: that file is fetched, its
  signature checked, and its SHA-256 compared with this bundle's `previous`, so a dropped or swapped minute shows;
- the pricing inputs and each asset's σ;
- the last settled Windows, each recomputed from its prints, and a button that recomputes every settlement in the
  last day's prints files, checking each print against the same files' 1-minute closes.

## By hand

1. Fetch `latest.json`, its bundle and an hour's `prints-YYYYMMDDHH.json` from the data host.
2. Check each `sig`: Ed25519 over the file's canonical JSON with `sig` removed, the signature in base64url.
3. For a Window: compare each exchange's closing print with its opening print (exact decimals), and take two of
   three. You can fetch each exchange's own 1-minute candles to check the prints themselves.
4. For a price: `ask = ⌈P·10⁴⌉ + 250` from the formula on [Rules](/rules), and `payout = ⌊stake·10⁴ / ask⌋` rounded
   down to 1,000 zatoshi.
