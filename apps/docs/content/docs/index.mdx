Kakure lets you call the close on crypto and on Zcash itself with shielded ZEC. You pay from your own Zcash
wallet by scanning a QR code. There is no account to make, nothing to sign in to, and no claim step: winnings and
refunds come back on their own to an address you choose.

The odds are public. Who placed each bet isn't. The precise boundary: **the public sees nothing beyond what any
shielded Zcash transaction shows, and the operator sees what was bet, never who bet it.**

## What's live

- **Testnet** markets run now, with test coins (TAZ) that have no value. Hourly and daily Up/Down markets on BTC,
  ETH, SOL and ZEC, daily ones on DOGE and XRP, and a testnet chain-height event.
- **Mainnet** opens once its operator key is published; until then the app says so instead of showing markets.
- **US stocks** open once their price feed is connected.
- The **web app** is live. The Android and iPhone apps are listed on the app's download page as each build is
  published.
