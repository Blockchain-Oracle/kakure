KAKURE — DEMO SCRIPT
One story, in the order a new user meets it. [Brackets] = where you are in the app. Don't read them.
About 351 spoken words: 1:53 at your pace, plus a few seconds of holds on the proof. Hackathon rule: two minutes.

1 · OPENING · 0:00
   [ZECATHON page → Kakure repo → the landing hero]

Howdy to the ZECATHON team and the zkSNARKs crew. It's Blockchain Oracle, and I bring to you Kakure — trade every
minute, stay hidden.

2 · THE PROBLEM · 0:07
   [A public prediction market's activity feed, an exchange sign-up form]

Just a quick question. When you bet on a prediction market, who knows it was you? On chain, your address. On an
exchange, your account, your email, your IP. Your best call is public the second you make it.

3 · THE SOLUTION · 0:20
   [Landing hero → Markets]

So I built Kakure. Bitcoin, Ether, Solana and Zcash, up or down, from a minute to a day, at a live price anyone can
check. The prices are public. You aren't. Let me show you, from the start.

4 · MONEY IN · 0:31
   [Portfolio → Deposit → address and memo → chips arrive]

No sign-up. I pay this shielded address from my Zcash wallet. Minutes later, I have chips,
blind-signed, so not even the mint knows which deposit they came from.

5 · ONE CALL · 0:44
   [Terminal → BTC → 5m Window → UP pays 2.36× → the position]

Now the fun part. Bitcoin, five-minute Window. Up pays two point three six times, right now. Watch this. One tap.
My browser seals the order and sends it through a relay: the relay sees my IP, not the order; the operator sees the
order, never my IP. I can close early, trail it, or let it ride.

6 · THE PROOF · 1:02
   [Settled → paid as chips → Proof → the Window → recomputed]

It settled, paid back in fresh chips. Let's confirm it. Proof takes the signed prints and recomputes the
outcome from three exchanges' own candles. It matches. You don't have to trust me.

7 · THE REST · 1:15
   [Parlay slip → Range → Games hall → Duel → Leaderboard, Bets → /privacy]

And that's just one call. Parlays chain up to three Windows; Range bets on where it closes. An arcade on real price
tapes, Lucky drawn on your own device, and a Duel with picks sealed until both lock in. Publish a call to the Bets
board for credit: right calls only, never your trade. And this page says exactly who sees what, even what we
can't hide.

8 · UNDER THE HOOD · 1:38
   [README architecture → Who sees what]

Under the hood, it's Zcash all the way: shielded deposits and payouts, a wallet syncing over Tor, e-cash chips, and
Oblivious HTTP.

Every rule and file format is in the docs. docs dot getkakure dot x y z. And follow us on X, at HANDLE.
